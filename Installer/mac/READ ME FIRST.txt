Mz950 for macOS
===============

An emulator that plays back Akai S900/S950 disk images: a VST3, an Audio Unit
(for Logic and GarageBand) and a standalone app. Free and open source (AGPLv3).
Independent - not affiliated with Akai or inMusic.

Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or later.


INSTALLING - one command
------------------------

macOS will refuse to open anything in this download if you double-click it.
That isn't a fault. Apple only lets through software whose developer has paid
for an Apple account and sent it to Apple to be checked ("notarization"), and
this project doesn't. So it's installed from Terminal instead:

  1. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal).
  2. Type  bash  and a space.
  3. Drag install.sh from this folder into the Terminal window.
  4. Press Return.

It copies everything into place inside your own home folder, with no password
needed, and clears the "downloaded from the internet" flag that would otherwise
stop your DAW loading the plugin. Then rescan plug-ins in your DAW.


INSTALLING BY HAND, if you'd rather
-----------------------------------

  Mz950.vst3        ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  Mz950.component   ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  Mz950.app         ->  /Applications
  Disks             ->  ~/Library/Application Support/Mz950/Disks

then, in Terminal, for each one you copied:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine <the file, dragged in>


REMOVING IT
-----------

Delete the four things above. Nothing else is installed anywhere.
